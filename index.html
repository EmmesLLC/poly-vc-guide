Meeting Room Guide

Poly / Polycom Video Conferencing

Pick what you need to do using the two tabs below.

📹 Run a meeting Teams / Zoom / Webex
🖥️ Screen on TV No call needed

Use the room's Poly camera, microphone and speaker for a Teams / Zoom / Webex call.

1

Connect the VC device

  1. Plug the USB cable from the Poly/Polycom VC device into your laptop.
  2. Ensure the device is powered ON.
  3. Connect the room display (TV/monitor) to the VC unit — usually HDMI, already set up in meeting rooms.
2

Join your meeting

  1. Open Teams / Zoom / Webex.
  2. Join the meeting normally.
3

Select the Poly devices in the call

No need to change settings beforehand — switch devices directly inside the meeting:

  1. In the call, click the small arrow (˅) next to the Mic, Camera, and Share / Screen-share buttons.
  2. From each dropdown, select the Poly device:
    • Camera: Poly Studio / Poly Camera
    • Microphone: Poly Studio / Poly Audio
    • Speaker: Poly Studio

Prefer to set it up before joining?

  1. In your meeting app, go to Settings → Devices.
  2. Set Camera, Microphone, and Speaker to Poly Studio.
4

Share your screen

In Microsoft Teams

  1. Click Share.
  2. Choose Entire screen (recommended), or Window / PowerPoint.
  3. Click Share.

In Zoom

  1. Click Share Screen.
  2. Select your screen / window.
  3. Click Share.
5

Display on the room screen

  1. The shared content should automatically appear on the TV connected to the Poly device.
If the content doesn't appear:
  • Check the TV input is set to the correct HDMI source.
  • Make sure your laptop is not incorrectly in “extend” display mode. If it is, either share the correct display inside the meeting app, or press ⊞ Win + P and choose Duplicate.
6

HDMI content shareOptional

Some rooms allow direct sharing without a meeting:

  1. Connect the HDMI cable from the table to your laptop.
  2. Press the Content / Share button on the Poly remote (if available).
  3. Your screen will display instantly.

For a local presentation with no meeting or call. Try the options in order — Option 1 works in most rooms.

1

HDMI direct sharingMost common

  1. Locate the HDMI cable on the meeting table.
  2. Connect the HDMI cable into your laptop.
  3. Your screen will either mirror automatically, or appear after the TV input is selected.
If the screen does NOT show:
  • Windows: press ⊞ Win + P, then choose Duplicate (mirror) or Extend.
  • Mac: go to System Settings → Displays and turn on Mirror Display.
2

USB “Device Mode” sharing

Use this if no HDMI cable is available.

  1. Connect the USB cable from the Poly device into your laptop.
  2. Make sure the TV is showing the Poly home screen.
  3. Then use one of the methods below.

Method A — Device / Room Mode

  • Some Poly systems auto-switch when USB is connected.
  • Your laptop display will then control the room system.

Method B — Built-in casting (if enabled)

  1. Press ⊞ Win + K.
  2. Select the meeting room display from the list (only works if wireless casting is enabled in the room).
3

Poly Content ShareIf available

Some rooms have a dedicated Poly Content Share device or button.

  1. Connect via HDMI or USB.
  2. Press the Content Share button.
  3. Your screen appears on the TV.
